Pricing

Free forever. Premium if you want to automate everything.

The free app is the real app — your hardware, your network, your server, no account and no CraftPanel cloud required. Premium adds the automation, monitoring, and management tools for people running more than one server, or who'd rather CraftPanel just handle it.

What's in each

Nothing about hosting your server is behind a paywall

Premium does not unlock the ability to host publicly, or anything else about running the server itself — that's the whole point of CraftPanel, and it stays free. Premium is extra tooling on top, for managing it with less manual work.
