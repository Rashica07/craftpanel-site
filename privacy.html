1. The short version

CraftPanel is self-hosted. Your Minecraft servers, worlds, player data, backups, and console logs live entirely on your own computer — CraftPanel does not upload them anywhere, and there is no CraftPanel-run cloud that sees them. The sections below cover the small amount of data involved in running the app itself and in a Premium purchase.

2. What the free app does and doesn't collect

  • No account. The free app doesn't require sign-up or a login.
  • No telemetry or analytics are sent from the app about your servers, worlds, or how you use it.
  • Update checks. The app checks GitHub's release feed to see if a newer version is available. This is a network request to GitHub (not to CraftPanel) and doesn't send any information identifying you.
  • Modrinth searches. Searching for mods, plugins, or modpacks sends your search text to Modrinth's public API, the same as visiting modrinth.com would.

3. What a Premium purchase involves

Premium checkout and billing are handled by Stripe. When you buy Premium:

  • Your payment details (card number, etc.) are entered directly into Stripe's checkout and are never seen or stored by CraftPanel.
  • CraftPanel receives, from Stripe, the information needed to issue and email you a license key: your email address, which plan you bought, and the payment status.
  • Your license key and the email it was issued to are stored so the app can validate the key and so a subscription renewal or lifetime purchase can be looked up if you need support.

Stripe's own privacy policy governs the payment data it collects directly: stripe.com/privacy.
